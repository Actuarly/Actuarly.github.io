## About Actuarly

Actuarly is an independent think tank writing on finance and policy issues of public interest. We take on complicated topics in finance and distill them into plain language that anyone can follow.

Our goal is to hold public institutions to account with a scientific, fact-based approach inspired by the actuarial sciences: clear assumptions, transparent methods, and honest uncertainty.

Every article is edited by Actuarly's editor board, and we only publish authors with substantial professional and academic experience in actuarial science, quantitative finance, or related fields.

## Our policy on AI

We believe the best way forward is to think with our brains and execute with AI. All decisions at Actuarly are taken by humans. Execution is carried out by humans and AI together. The judgement, the conclusions, and the responsibility for what we publish always stay with our authors and editors.

## Get in touch

All submissions are reviewed by the Actuarly editors. We especially welcome contributions from members of the actuarial profession and related disciplines, but we invite submissions from all backgrounds. If you have a well-founded perspective on finance or policy, we'd like to read it.
